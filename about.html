About the concept

Thoughtful care, designed around you.

Molar & Mint is a sample dental discovery website concept focused on a calm, friendly and easy-to-use patient experience.

Clear information. Comfortable choices.

The concept helps visitors explore common dental services, view sample clinics and walk through an appointment request without a complicated process.

All clinic names, fees, ratings, availability and booking confirmations on this static website are illustrative demo content, not real medical services.
